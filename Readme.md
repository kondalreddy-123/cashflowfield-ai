# CashFlowShield AI

## Predict the Cash Leak. Protect the Cash Before It Leaves.

CashFlowShield AI is an AI-powered cash-flow risk and early-warning system designed to help businesses identify potential cash-flow pressure before it happens.

## Problem

Businesses have money coming in and going out at different times.

They may face:

* Delayed customer payments
* Pending receivables
* RTO-related costs
* Supplier payments
* EMI payments
* Subscription and recurring expenses
* Unexpected cash-flow pressure

A business may have enough money today but still face a shortage when future payments become due.

## Solution

CashFlowShield AI analyzes business cash-flow information and provides an early view of potential cash-flow risks.

The system helps identify:

* Cash at risk
* RTO exposure
* Receivable risk
* Upcoming payments
* Future cash balance
* Cash-flow pressure
* Root causes
* Recommended actions

## How It Works

```text
Business Data
      ↓
Cash-Flow Analysis
      ↓
Risk Detection
      ↓
Cash-at-Risk
      ↓
Future Cash Forecast
      ↓
Early Warning
      ↓
Why? / Root Cause
      ↓
Recommended Actions
```

## Key Features

### Cash-at-Risk

Estimates money that may be exposed to loss or delay.

### RTO Risk

Estimates potential cash exposure caused by returned orders.

### Receivable Risk

Highlights pending customer payments.

### Upcoming Payments

Tracks expected payments such as suppliers, EMI, subscriptions, and recurring expenses.

### Future Cash Forecast

Shows the estimated future cash position.

### Early Warning

Alerts the business when potential cash-flow pressure is detected.

### What-If Analysis

Allows users to test scenarios such as:

* Higher RTO rate
* Lower customer inflow
* Higher expenses
* Faster receivable collection

### AI Recommendations

Provides suggested actions based on the detected risks.

## Important Disclaimer

CashFlowShield AI is a prototype for cash-flow analysis and decision support.

Predictions are estimates based on the information provided by the user or simulated data. They are not guaranteed financial advice.

The system does not automatically transfer, withdraw, block, or move money.

Users should only use authorized or simulated business data. Never upload passwords, PINs, OTPs, CVVs, or banking credentials.
